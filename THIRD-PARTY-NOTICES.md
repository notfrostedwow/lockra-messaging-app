lockfile and docs/evidence/dependencies-final.json record resolved versions and

metadata inspection did not establish their author or redistribution rights.

fonts/icons/dependencies before publication or distribution of built bundles.

data. They are not mockups or evidence of device/provider integration. Any legacy
art visible within them remains subject to the same pending rights review.
